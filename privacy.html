Push 'n Zoom Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version

Push 'n Zoom doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, the player name you type, your courses, car designs and replays are saved only on this device (and in your device's own backups). Deleting the game deletes them, and Settings → Reset progress erases your progress.

Camera (AR Zone)

AR Zone uses the camera live to place the game in your room. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Motion sensors (Motion Power)

Motion Power reads the motion sensors live while you rev. Nothing is stored.

Photos (Save Video)

The game can only add the replay videos you choose to save. It can't see your photo library.

Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and trophies go to Apple's Game Center so you and your friends can see them. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center. The game never sees your contacts.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details. Purchases in the game sit behind a grown-up check.

Age information

Where the law requires it, Apple may tell the game a player's age range (for example "under 13"). It stays on this device and is never shared.

Sharing course codes

A course code only leaves the game when a grown-up chooses to share it. It holds the course layout, its name and the author name. Names on codes from other players are checked for rude words and links before they're shown.

Children

Push 'n Zoom is made for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. Purchases, sharing and saving videos are behind a grown-up check.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and on our website. Questions? Email pushnzoom@gmail.com.
