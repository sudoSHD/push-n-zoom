Push 'n Zoom Support

Need help? Email pushnzoom@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Put a finger on the screen and rub back and forth to rev the toy car's flywheel, then lift your finger to launch. In the air, hold the left or right side of the screen to lean the car and land flat.

What's free, and what's the Full Garage?

Chapters 1–3 are free to play, along with Free Play, the Daily Challenge and the Rookie Cup in those rooms. The Full Garage is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything else: 10 more rooms and their levels, cars, drivers, paints, decals, parts and stickers, every cup, Party mode and more. There are no ads and no subscriptions. The optional packs only change how things look.

I bought something but it's missing

Go to Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Purchases work with Family Sharing.

The game asks a maths question before buying

That's the grown-ups-only check. It appears before any purchase, before sharing a course and before saving a video, so children can't do those things alone. Type the answer on the keypad and tap OK.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Motion Power safety

Motion Power lets you rev by pushing your phone like a toy car. Always grip the phone firmly, launch by lifting your thumb, and never throw or let go of the phone. Push it along a table if you like, keep clear of people, pets and breakable things, and kids should ask a grown-up first. You can switch back to Touch Rev any time.

AR Zone

AR Zone launches cars across your real floor using the camera. It needs an iPhone that supports ARKit and good light. Move the phone slowly until it finds the floor, then tap to place the launch pad.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, cars and courses are saved on your device. They're included in your device's backups. Deleting the game deletes them.
